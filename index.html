About Me

I started out solving small logic puzzles for fun and slowly turned that habit into a discipline, reading a problem carefully, breaking it down, and refusing to stop until the judge says "Accepted." That same mindset now shapes how I approach software: understand the problem fully before writing a single line of code.

Outside of contests, I build small full-stack projects to stay close to how real products are actually shipped- from the interface someone sees, down to the code that keeps it running.

Beyond academics and problem solving, I also have an entrepreneurial side. I run my own business, Rupedia, which gives me the opportunity to explore creativity, business, and leadership alongside technology. Building something of my own has taught me how to take responsibility, make decisions, and turn ideas into reality.
